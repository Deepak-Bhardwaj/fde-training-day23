# Maritime Context-Aware AI Case — Design Specification

Goal: rebuild the original maritime FDE case around the eight-layer context-aware AI architecture while preserving maritime command authority, edge/offline continuity and source-case evidence.

The repo must be self-contained, synthetic, evidence-rich, explicit about source-case benchmark vs 140-row fixture, and must not require Qwen. It must provide 15 deterministic failure/edge scenarios and 15 participant templates.
