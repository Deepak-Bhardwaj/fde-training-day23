# Demo Scenario Sequence

For an architecture-first demo, use this order:

1. **MFD-L001** — establish the nominal context/retrieval/recovery flow.
2. **MFD-L011** — prove the KG/context can retain conflicting berth evidence.
3. **MFD-L002** — prove AI remains decision support under severe-weather navigation impact.
4. **MFD-L005** — prove stale source behavior.
5. **MFD-L007** — prove event idempotency/replay handling.
6. **MFD-L009** — prove untrusted-content/prompt-injection isolation.
7. **MFD-L014** — prove offline vessel continuity.
8. **MFD-L015** — prove reconnect reconciliation and governed feedback.

Finish by opening the Decision Trace and Traceability Matrix rather than ending on the AI text output.
