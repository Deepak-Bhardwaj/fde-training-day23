# Application Screen Requirements

1. **Fleet Operations Control Tower** — 15 cases, severity, voyage/destination, source health, connectivity, deadlines.
2. **Voyage Context View** — vessel/voyage, AIS/telemetry, port/weather, cargo/crew/CMMS constraints, times/freshness.
3. **Evidence Reconciliation** — source observations, canonical mappings, identity/conflict warnings and provenance.
4. **Context Graph Explorer** — connected task slice with nodes/relationships and source/time metadata.
5. **Hybrid Retrieval Evidence** — structured/graph/vector/policy/memory tabs or badges with filters/results.
6. **Recovery Option Workbench** — candidate options, feasibility, blocking/conditional constraints, evidence and uncertainty.
7. **Authority & Policy Gate** — active policy version, triggered deterministic rules and required roles.
8. **Offline / Reconnect View** — connectivity state, queued events, dedupe keys, stale shore context and reconciliation.
9. **Decision Trace / Audit** — reconstructable trace with versions and human/technical decision.
10. **Outcome & Feedback** — voyage outcome, operator interaction and governed-learning status.
11. **Scenario / Failure Lab** — all 15 golden scenarios and evidence of pass/fail.
