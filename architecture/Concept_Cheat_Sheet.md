# Context-Aware AI Architecture — Concept Cheat Sheet

**Enterprise sources** provide raw observations, documents, APIs, streams and expert/policy knowledge.  
**Semantic layer** standardizes business meaning: entities, metrics, dimensions and definitions.  
**Ontology** defines domain concepts, relationships, taxonomies and semantic constraints.  
**Knowledge graph** connects canonical entities, facts, relationships, time and provenance.  
**Graph platform** stores/traverses those relationships and supports graph queries/analytics.  
**Hybrid retrieval** routes between structured, graph, vector, policy and controlled decision-memory retrieval.  
**Runtime context graph** builds the task-relevant connected slice for the current vessel/voyage, actor, time, source health, policy, history and provenance.  
**AI/agent** reasons over retrieved evidence, uses bounded tools and proposes options; it is not the authority.  
**Governance gates** enforce access, safety, policy and human decision rights.  
**Evidence/feedback** captures trace, outcomes, drift, quality and governed learning.

**Storyline:** Data → Meaning → Relationships → Retrieval → Context → Reasoning → Controlled Action → Feedback.

**Key principle:** The LLM is not the architecture.
