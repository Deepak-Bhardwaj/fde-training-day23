# Architecture Challenge

Design a production-oriented target architecture for the **Fleet Disruption & Voyage Recovery Intelligence Workbench** using the eight-layer storyline in the reference visual.

Your design must explain how heterogeneous vessel/shore evidence becomes canonical meaning, connected knowledge, task-specific context and controlled human decision support. It must continue essential vessel workflows during disconnection and must not turn a prototype chatbot into autonomous ship authority.

Required architecture evidence:

1. source contracts, authority, permissions, freshness and degraded behavior;
2. semantic layer and ontology;
3. entity resolution and provenance-preserving KG;
4. graph-platform ADR and representative traversals;
5. hybrid retrieval router/fusion strategy;
6. runtime context-graph contract and invalidation;
7. bounded AI/agent tools + deterministic policy/human gates;
8. decision trace, evals, monitoring and governed feedback loop.
