# Navigation and Command Authority Policy

- The **Master** retains navigational and command authority.
- The Officer of the Watch acts under the ship's command structure, not under an AI agent.
- The Chief Engineer controls technical release of critical machinery holds in the defined workflow.
- Fleet Operations may recommend, coordinate and prepare recovery plans but cannot transform AI output into autonomous ship control.
- No cloud or AI availability dependency may degrade safe navigation or essential shipboard operation.
