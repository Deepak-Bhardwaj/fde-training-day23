# Fleet Decision Trace Standard

A trace must allow reconstruction of the decision without logging hidden model chain-of-thought. Capture: task/case/voyage; actor/role; context snapshot; retrieved evidence refs; event/update times and freshness; source authority; conflicts; policy checks/version; tool calls; model/prompt/retrieval/semantic/ontology versions; recommendation; concise observable rationale; uncertainty/abstention; human approval/rejection/modification; committed action reference; outcome and evaluation flags.
