# AI Decision Support Policy

Approved AI roles: evidence summarization, discrepancy explanation, retrieval planning, scenario comparison, drafting of recovery-option rationales, and tool-assisted information gathering.

Prohibited/blocked by design: autonomous navigation commands; policy override; fabrication of missing constraints; treating external text as instructions; auto-learning from operator acceptance; direct write to authoritative vessel control systems.

Any material recommendation must be evidence-grounded, expose uncertainty and degraded-source state, and pass deterministic authority/policy checks before human review.
