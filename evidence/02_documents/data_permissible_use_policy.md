# Data Permissible-Use Policy

- Vessel telemetry: operational use permitted within fleet purpose; preserve source/sequence/time.
- AIS and third-party weather/ocean/port data: conditional on provider license/purpose and source freshness.
- Cargo/customer data: restricted, purpose-minimized, and customer/voyage isolated.
- Crew data: restricted personal data; use only the minimum operational constraint needed for the task.
- Navigation command/control outputs: prohibited as AI write authority in this case.
- Unknown/conditional/restricted never means allowed by default.
